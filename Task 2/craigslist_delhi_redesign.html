Heuristic Evaluation

Evaluated against Nielsen's 10 usability heuristics, based on the craigslist.org/area/delhi homepage. Severity: 3 = major, 2 = moderate, 1 = minor.

1 · Visibility of System Status
Severity 2
Issue
No timestamps, "new listings" counts, or activity indicators on the homepage — a user can't tell if the board is active or stale.
Recommendation
Surface a live "X new today" count per category and relative timestamps on any listing preview.
2 · Match Between System & Real World
Severity 3
Issue
Category labels use insider shorthand ("atv/utv/sno", "sm biz ads", "w2w") that a first-time or non-native-English visitor in Delhi won't parse.
Recommendation
Replace abbreviations with plain-language labels and icons; group into a handful of recognizable top-level categories.
3 · User Control & Freedom
Severity 2
Issue
No visible way to change city, undo a filter, or return to the homepage without using the browser back button.
Recommendation
Persistent nav with logo-as-home, an explicit city switcher, and breadcrumbs on category pages.
4 · Consistency & Standards
Severity 2
Issue
Every link — navigation, category, and primary action — is styled identically (same blue, same weight), so nothing signals importance.
Recommendation
Establish a real hierarchy: filled buttons for actions, tinted cards for categories, plain text for tertiary links.
5 · Error Prevention
Severity 1
Issue
"post an ad" gives no preview of what's required before a user commits to the flow, risking abandoned drafts.
Recommendation
Show a short "what you'll need" checklist (photos, price, location) before entering the posting flow.
6 · Recognition Rather Than Recall
Severity 3
Issue
Roughly 100 text links compete for attention with no icons, images, or grouping cues — users must read and remember, not recognize.
Recommendation
Use icon + color-coded category tiles and thumbnail images on listing previews so scanning replaces reading.
7 · Flexibility & Efficiency of Use
Severity 2
Issue
No saved searches, filters, or recently viewed shortcuts for returning users — every visit starts from zero.
Recommendation
Add quick-filter chips (price, category, distance) and a "recently viewed" row for logged-in users.
8 · Aesthetic & Minimalist Design
Severity 3
Issue
The page is a dense wall of same-weight text with almost no whitespace, imagery, or visual grouping — cognitively heavy to scan.
Recommendation
Introduce whitespace, a clear grid, and a small set of top categories above the fold, with the long tail one click away.
9 · Help Users Recognize & Recover from Errors
Severity 1
Issue
No visible empty-state or error messaging patterns on the homepage to evaluate against, which itself signals this hasn't been designed for.
Recommendation
Design explicit empty/error states (no results, expired listing) that explain what happened and offer a next step.
10 · Help & Documentation
Severity 2
Issue
"avoid scams & fraud" and "personal safety tips" are buried as small low-contrast links at the page bottom, despite being high-value trust content.
Recommendation
Promote safety/help content into the footer as a clearly labeled group, and link it contextually from the post-ad flow.

Mood Board

Direction: "hand-painted market signage." Delhi's local markets — Chandni Chowk, Lajpat Nagar — communicate through bold flat-color signboards with thick outlines and hard shadows, not glossy tech gradients. That vernacular gives the redesign warmth and locality while staying legible and modern.
